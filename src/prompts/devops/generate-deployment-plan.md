## Requirements

Generate a comprehensive production deployment plan that includes:
1. **Strategy**: Deployment strategy (rolling update, blue-green, canary, etc.)
2. **Steps**: Ordered deployment steps with automation flags
3. **Rollback Plan**: Trigger conditions and rollback steps
4. **Monitoring**: Health checks, alerts, and dashboards
5. **Risks**: Potential risks with severity and mitigation strategies

The plan should ensure zero-downtime deployment with proper safety mechanisms.
